FieldShift — Global Earth Observation & Agricultural Intelligence Platform

An integrated platform for Earth observation, climate intelligence, environmental monitoring, and science-based agricultural decision support.

<p align="center">
  <strong>Global Research • Satellite Data • Climate Analytics • Agricultural Intelligence</strong>
</p>Overview

FieldShift is a global Earth observation and agricultural intelligence platform designed to help users explore environmental conditions, understand climate-related agricultural risks, and make more informed land and farming decisions.

The platform aims to connect Earth observation datasets, meteorological information, environmental indicators, and scientific analysis within an accessible research and decision-support environment.

Although developed with agricultural applications in mind, FieldShift is designed for geographical exploration and analysis across different regions of the world.

Vision

To make scientific Earth observation and environmental intelligence more accessible, understandable, and useful for agricultural adaptation, climate resilience, and environmental research.

Core Capabilities

1. Global Earth Observation

- Interactive geographical exploration.
- Regional and global environmental monitoring.
- Satellite-derived information and geospatial visualization.
- Exploration of land, vegetation, and atmospheric conditions.

2. Climate and Weather Intelligence

- Temperature and humidity monitoring.
- Precipitation and rainfall analysis.
- Wind and atmospheric indicators.
- Historical climate exploration.
- Weather forecasting where supported by connected providers.

3. Agricultural Intelligence

- Crop suitability and agricultural conditions.
- Crop-calendar and seasonal planning support.
- Irrigation and water-management analysis.
- Agricultural climate-risk assessment.
- Climate-adaptive farming recommendations.

4. Soil, Water and Environmental Analysis

- Soil and land-condition information where datasets are available.
- Water availability and agricultural water-demand assessment.
- Vegetation monitoring.
- Environmental and drought-related indicators.

5. Research and Scientific Exploration

- Data-driven environmental analysis.
- Historical and spatial comparisons.
- Research-oriented visualization.
- Scientific data exploration and interpretation.
- Transparent dataset sources and methodology.

FieldShift uses a web-based architecture and integrates external scientific data services.

The exact technology stack, APIs, infrastructure, and dependencies should be documented according to the actual implementation in this repository.

Scientific Integrity

FieldShift aims to follow these principles:

1. Prefer authoritative and traceable data sources.
2. Distinguish observed data from model forecasts.
3. Display units, timestamps, and geographical coverage.
4. Communicate uncertainty and data limitations.
5. Avoid unsupported agricultural or scientific claims.
6. Treat AI-generated recommendations as decision support, not guaranteed outcomes.
7. Protect user information and API credentials.

Responsible Use

FieldShift is an informational and research-oriented decision-support platform. It does not replace professional agricultural advice, laboratory soil testing, official weather warnings, or local agricultural authorities.

Users should independently verify critical farming, irrigation, fertilizer, and pesticide decisions.
